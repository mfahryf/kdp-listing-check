KDP Listing Checker

Paste your book's title, description, keywords and categories. See what breaks Amazon KDP's published rules before you publish.

Runs entirely in your browser. Your listing is not uploaded anywhere; it is only remembered on this device so you can come back to it.

    FAIL is certain: a count over a limit, an unsupported tag, a link. WARN is a word match that needs your judgement: "free" in the title Free Fall is fine. Each finding links to the KDP help page it comes from.

    What it checks

    Title, subtitle, seriesTitle and subtitle together must be fewer than 200 characters. No sales-rank words ("bestselling"), no promotional words, no HTML, no placeholders, no repeated generic words. KDP notes that customers skim past titles longer than 60 characters.
    Description4,000 characters at most, and HTML tags count. Only these tags are supported: <br> <p> <b> <em> <i> <u> <h4> <h5> <h6> <ol> <ul> <li>. No website addresses, emails or phone numbers, no reviews or quotes, no requests for reviews, no prices, no time-sensitive information, no emoji, no keyword lists.
    KeywordsUp to seven. Avoid words already in your title, author name or categories, subjective claims ("best"), time-sensitive words ("new"), words common to everything in the store ("book"), Amazon program names, quotation marks, and spelling, spacing or plural variants of each other.
    CategoriesUp to three.

    The rules come from three KDP help pages, checked on 11 November 2026: Metadata Guidelines for Books, Make Your Book More Discoverable with Keywords and Write a Book Description. KDP can change them. If a finding looks wrong, the linked page wins.

    What it cannot tell you

    • Which keywords shoppers type, or whether your book will rank. It has no Amazon search data. To test a keyword, type it into Amazon's search bar and look at the suggestions and the first page of results.
    • Whether a keyword names another author or a brand you do not own, or whether your categories fit your book. Those need a person.
    • Whether KDP will accept the listing. Passing every check is not a guarantee.
